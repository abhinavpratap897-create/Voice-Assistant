    🔒 Privacy and security

    • No data is shared with any app. Before this assistant opens any installed app, it asks you to allow or deny, every time.
    • Only an app's name and link are saved, in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to any server by this page.
    • This page cannot read your messages, contacts, photos or accounts, and never asks for passwords.
    • Your chat is not saved. It disappears when you close the page.
    • Voice input uses your browser's speech service (Chrome and Edge send audio to their provider). Typing avoids that. Searches, weather and news open Google in a new tab, which receives that search.
    • On a shared device, erase your data when you are done.
